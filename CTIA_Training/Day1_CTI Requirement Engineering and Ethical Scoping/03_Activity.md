## Step 3: Complete Section 1 - Request Completeness Audit

## Step 4: Complete Section 2 - Legal Risk Identification

- Read the raw request carefully.
- Identify at least **3 distinct legal risks**. This is the minimum; you may find more.
- For each risk, name the specific law or policy it violates using the reference list below.
- Assign a `High`, `Med`, or `Low` risk level with a one-sentence justification.

### Reference: Applicable Laws

Use these for Section 2:

- **CFAA (Computer Fraud and Abuse Act):** Prohibits unauthorized access to computers.
- **GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation):** Prohibits collection of personal data without consent.
- **CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act):** Restricts sale or sharing of personal information.
- **Computer Misuse Act (UK/Commonwealth):** Similar to the CFAA.
- **Organizational Acceptable Use Policy:** Internal rules about data collection.
- **Terms of Service Violation:** Even if not a law, this is still a legal contract violation.

## Step 5: Complete Section 3 - Privacy Constraint Identification

- Identify specific privacy concerns. Minimum: **2**.
- Name the exact data element at risk, such as `home address`, `real name`, or `employment history`.
- State one specific mitigation, such as `redact before delivery`, `exclude entirely`, or `obtain written consent`.

## Step 6: Complete Section 4 - Express Scope Definition

### In Scope

List **3-5** appropriate intelligence activities that would be legal and ethical, for example:

- analyze publicly available threat reports about GlobalCorp's incident
- monitor OSINT sources for mentions of the suspected actor

### Out of Scope

List **3-5** items from the raw request that you are explicitly rejecting, for example:

- collection of real names and home addresses
- use of former employee for internal access

## Step 7: Complete Section 5 - Signed Approval

- Write your name and the current date.
- Check **one** of the three action boxes based on your analysis.

Use this decision rule:

- Check **Proceed** only if the request has no legal or ethical violations after scoping.
- Check **Return to requestor** if missing information prevents safe scoping.
- Check **Reject entirely** if the request fundamentally violates law or policy.

For this specific request, the correct answer is **Reject entirely**.
